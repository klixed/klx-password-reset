Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

KLX Trade Tracker ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal trading journal. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is stored, and the choices you have.

1. Data you provide

  • Account identifier — an email address or chosen username, plus a password you set.
  • Trade entries — the pairs, prices, levels, outcomes, notes, tags, and attachments you add.

2. Data we collect automatically

  • Session tokens issued by our authentication provider (Supabase) so you can stay signed in.
  • Standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, request path) retained for a short period for abuse prevention.

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. How your data is stored

When you are signed in, your trades are stored in a Postgres database hosted by Supabase, under row-level security policies that let only your authenticated user read or write your rows. When you are signed out, trades you add are kept only on your device.

4. Market price data

To auto-fill the price at the time of your trade, the app queries public market-data providers (including Yahoo Finance, Binance, and CoinGecko) with the pair symbol and timestamp you entered. These requests do not include your account identifier.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties except as needed to operate the authentication and database services the app depends on, or where required by law.

6. Your choices

  • Export: tap Export on the Trades screen to download your trades as CSV.
  • Delete a trade: open it and tap Delete trade.
  • Delete your account: email the address below. We will delete your account and all associated trades within 30 days.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions, data requests, or deletion requests: send an email to the address listed in the app store listing for KLX Trade Tracker.
